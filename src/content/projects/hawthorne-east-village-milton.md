## Where it is

Hawthorne East Village is in Milton, with GO Transit, local transit and Highways 401 and 407 close by. Milton's parks and conservation areas are a short drive away, including the Bruce Trail, Rattlesnake Point and Glen Eden, along with Toronto Premium Outlets and the Milton Farmers' Market.

## Homes you can move into soon

The builder lists several quick move-in homes, both townhomes and detached, with some ready now and others ready through early 2027. Availability changes week to week, so ask us for the current list.

## What Net Zero Ready means

The two-storey town and detached homes are built to the Canadian Home Builders' Association Net Zero Ready standard: more insulation and more efficient windows than a minimum building-code home, and set up for solar panels to be added later.
