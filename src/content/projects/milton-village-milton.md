## Where it is

Milton Village is a master-planned community in Milton, with easy access to GO Transit and Highways 401 and 407, and with parks and green space close by.

## The homes

Great Gulf is selling two-storey freehold townhomes here, each with a backyard. Freehold means you own the home and the land, with no condo corporation.

- **The Clarke** has three bedrooms, a U-shaped kitchen open to the great room, and a primary bedroom with an ensuite and walk-in closet.
- **The Briar** has four bedrooms and three full washrooms upstairs, an L-shaped kitchen with an island and walk-in pantry, and a primary bedroom with an ensuite and walk-in closet.

Both have upstairs laundry and a basement with a rough-in for a future washroom.
