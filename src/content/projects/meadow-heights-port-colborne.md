## Where it is

Meadow Heights is on Hillcrest Road in Port Colborne, in the Niagara Region. By car it is about:

- 5 minutes to the shops and restaurants of historic West Street
- 6 minutes to Nickel Beach and the Lake Erie waterfront
- 15 minutes to Welland for shopping, schools and the hospital
- 20 minutes to the QEW by Highway 140
- 30 minutes to Niagara Falls

## About the prices

Base prices are from the builder's April 24, 2026 price list. Lot premiums are extra.

## Worth asking us about

- **Deposit structure and lot availability.** These are on the builder's full price list, which we can send you.
- **HST.** The builder advertises that no HST is added to the base price on qualifying new homes. Eligibility varies by buyer, so confirm yours before you sign.
- **The rental suite option** on the semi-detached plans, if you are buying as an investment.
