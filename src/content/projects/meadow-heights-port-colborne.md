## Where it is

Meadow Heights is on Hillcrest Road in Port Colborne, in the Niagara Region. By car it is about:

- 5 minutes to the shops and restaurants of historic West Street
- 6 minutes to Nickel Beach and the Lake Erie waterfront
- 15 minutes to Welland for shopping, schools and the hospital
- 20 minutes to the QEW by Highway 140
- 30 minutes to Niagara Falls
